FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

Do you charge dealer fees?

No. Every sale is a private-party transaction, so there are no doc fees, prep fees or add-ons. The price on the card is the price.

Can I see what work was done before I buy?

Yes. Ask for the car's work summary: it lists every repair, part replaced and fluid service we completed, and a copy goes home with the car.

What does "As-Is" mean here?

Our cars are sold as-is, without a dealer warranty. What we promise instead is honesty: if something is worn or needs attention, we tell you upfront and price the car accordingly.

What does "In Reconditioning" mean?

We own the car and are still working on it. The card shows what's done and what's in progress. Text us to get first dibs before it's listed as available.

Is this a good place to buy a first car for my teenager?

That's a big part of why we do this. Every car is mechanically vetted and fully documented, and you're dealing with the family that did the work, not a sales floor.

Can my mechanic inspect the car?

Yes. Text us to set up a time for a pre-purchase inspection with the mechanic of your choice.
